Abyss of Tears — Original audio, 0.10.0

The score, motifs, arrangements, synthesis and effects in assets/audio were
created for this project using tools/compose_soundtrack.py. No third-party
audio samples, songs or game recordings are included.

These compositions, recordings and their source are distributed under the
project MIT license (see LICENSE). Godot has its separate MIT notices.

The first collection covers the existing three-floor campaign; tracks for
the proposed twelve-floor expansion remain to be composed and integrated.
